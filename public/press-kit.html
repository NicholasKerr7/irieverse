Feature Story

IrieVerse turns scattered Jamaica trip inspiration into a focused planning flow: discover, save, map, build, export, and share.

Discover by vibe

Browse places and experiences by region, rating, cost, energy, and travel vibe.

Map the route

Preview route order, drive time, distance, route days, and selected stops.

Save ideas

Keep places, experiences, pasted links, notes, and future social ideas in one place.

Build the trip

Plan dates, budget, route pacing, flights, nearby events, and booking recommendations.

Export and share

Export ICS calendar files and create share links when Supabase is configured.

Fallback-safe demos

The app still runs with local data when optional live APIs are missing.
