# Local Mode Plan

IrieVerse supports people who live in Jamaica, not only visitors planning a vacation. The local angle is strongest when the app becomes a weekend, day-trip, hosting, and island-movement planner.

## Positioning

**Local mode:** Plan weekends, food runs, beach days, river days, events, and hosting routes around Jamaica.

The app should still keep the Jamaica-first route, weather, saved-ideas, and sharing advantage, but the language should shift from "vacation itinerary" to "what are we doing this weekend, and how realistic is the route?"

## Core Local Use Cases

- Save places from TikTok, Instagram, Google Maps, YouTube, articles, and notes into Jamaica boards.
- Plan weekend runs like Kingston to Portland, Mobay to Negril, Ochi to Blue Hole, and South Coast day trips.
- Check realistic road pacing before committing to too many stops.
- Build clean plans for friends, family, dates, group trips, or visitors being hosted by locals.
- Track food spots, river days, parties, pop-ups, events, hidden gems, and repeat favorites.
- Share a simple plan with a group instead of sending scattered links.
- Use weather-aware planning for beach, river, falls, and outdoor days.

## Product Changes

- Add an onboarding choice: "I am visiting Jamaica" or "I live in Jamaica." Shipped through the Home and Trips planning mode selector.
- Let local users set a home parish, common base, or current location. Shipped through starting-area selection and optional exact GPS.
- Default local mode to JMD budgeting while still allowing USD. Shipped through the Trips budget controls.
- Add day-trip and weekend-trip templates. Shipped through local food run and river/beach templates.
- Add "near me this weekend" and "within 60/90/120 minutes" planning views. Shipped in the Quick Plan local controls.
- Add local event and pop-up discovery as a first-class Saved/Explore source.
- Add group planning for friends: shared board, quick vote, and final plan.
- Add route handoff to Waze or Google Maps for actual driving.

## UX Direction

Local mode should feel faster and less formal than trip planning:

- "Plan this weekend" instead of "Build itinerary."
- "Start near me" instead of "Choose airport/base."
- "Food run," "River day," "Beach day," "Date night," and "Host visitors" templates.
- Shorter plans: half-day, full-day, overnight, weekend.
- More emphasis on drive time, weather, opening hours, and event timing.

## Not In Scope Yet

- In-app turn-by-turn navigation.
- Live traffic replacement for Waze or Google Maps.
- Full social network features.
- Payments, reservations, or ticketing.
